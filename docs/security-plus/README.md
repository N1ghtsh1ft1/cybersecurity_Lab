# Security+

Notes and study material for the CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) certification.

Written for recall rather than coverage — mnemonics, comparison tables, and common exam traps, with a self-check section at the end of every file.

## Contents

**[Domain 1: General Security Concepts](domain-1-general-security-concepts.md)** carries a weight of 12%, covering foundational terminology, the CIA triad, AAA, control types and categories, Zero Trust, and cryptography fundamentals.

**[Domain 2: Threats, Vulnerabilities & Mitigations](domain-2-threats-vulnerabilities-mitigations.md)** carries a weight of 22%, covering threat actors and motivations, social engineering, malware types, vulnerability classes, indicators of malicious activity, and mitigation techniques.

**[Domain 3: Security Architecture](domain-3-security-architecture.md)** carries a weight of 18%, covering architecture models, secure infrastructure and failure modes, secure communication, data protection and classification, and resilience and recovery.

**[Domain 4: Security Operations](domain-4-security-operations.md)** carries a weight of 28%, covering incident response, digital forensics, log sources, monitoring and alerting, vulnerability management, hardening and baselines, and identity and access management.

**[Domain 5: Security Program Management & Oversight](domain-5-program-management-oversight.md)** carries a weight of 20%, covering governance, risk management and the SLE/ALE formulas, third-party risk, compliance, audits and assessments, and security awareness.

Domain 4 carries the most weight and maps most closely to Tier 1 SOC work.

## How to use these

Read the note, then cover the page and answer the self-check questions out loud. Anything you can't answer without looking is the thing to restudy — not the material you already found easy.
